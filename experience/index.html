    Researcher — Department of Environmental Sciences

    Mizoram University, Aizawl, India

    • Assess forest loss driven by shifting cultivation in Northeast India using multi-temporal satellite imagery and field data.

    • Mapped forest canopy density change and land surface temperature (LST) in Kolasib district to quantify canopy loss and its thermal signature.
    • Developed a hybrid machine learning–RUSLE workflow to map soil erosion risk in Aizawl district.

    Project Assistant — Research Projects

    Nalanda University & Mizoram University, India

    1. Mapping of Understory Bamboo Diversity in Arunachal Pradesh using Geospatial Technology and Artificial Neural Network

    • Contributed to developing and validating an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) model in Google Earth Engine to map bamboo at species level.
    • Processed multi-temporal satellite imagery and used field reference data for classification and validation.

    2. Identification and Mapping of Important Wetlands of Arunachal Pradesh for RAMSAR Convention Eligibility using Geo-Spatial Technologies

    • Delineated important wetlands from remote sensing data.
    • Assessed wetland characteristics against Ramsar criteria to support conservation policy recommendations.

    3. Assessment of Environmental Carrying Capacity of Dampa Tiger Reserve (DTR), Mizoram

    • Collected field ecological data across the reserve and analysed the carrying capacity of its ecosystem.
    • Helped prepare investigation reports with management recommendations.

    4. Mosquito Breeding Habitat Mapping

    • Integrated field data, satellite imagery and GIS to map larval habitats and malaria transmission risk.
